## Join Us for CSMN Fellowship

We are excited to invite you to our CSMN fellowship this Friday evening at our home base. Every week, we gather for a warm and joyful time of food, friendship, and community.

We meet at 18:00, and the evening begins with a delicious Chinese dinner that we share together. This is a wonderful opportunity to enjoy great food, connect with familiar faces, and welcome new Chinese students who have signed up.

To help us prepare enough food for everyone, please sign up in advance. Your registration helps us plan well and care for every guest.

After our fellowship dinner, we often enjoy birthday cake or a special dessert to celebrate something meaningful. With coffee, tea, soft drinks, or water, we continue the evening with a special time of singing together, followed by a study. Each week, the study focuses on a topic that helps us explore and discover life in a deeper and more thoughtful way.

For newcomers, we also offer a gentle introduction group so you can get to know us step by step.

You can sign up here: [events](/en/events).

Come and be part of a beautiful community. Good food, meaningful conversations, and new friendships are waiting for you. We look forward to seeing you this Friday!
