## Join us for CSMN Fellowship

We are excited to invite you to our CSMN fellowship this Friday evening at our home base. Every week we gather for a warm and joyful time of food, friendship, and community.

We meet at 18:00. The evening begins with a Chinese dinner that we share together. It is a relaxed opportunity to enjoy good food, connect with familiar faces, and welcome new Chinese students.

After dinner we often enjoy birthday cake or a special dessert, then continue with singing and a study. Each week the study focuses on a topic that helps us explore life, faith, and deeper questions together.

For newcomers, we also offer a gentle introduction group so you can get to know us step by step.

You can sign up through the [events page](/en/events).
