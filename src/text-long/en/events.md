## CSMN Events: stay connected all year long

Throughout the year we organize events where students can meet, ask questions, eat together, and discover life in the Netherlands. Bookmark this page to stay updated.

Here is a taste of what happens during the year:

**Hiking with Chinese students**  
Exploring nature in and around Wageningen.

**Movie nights**  
A moment away with a thought-provoking movie and conversation.

**Hot Pot Night**  
Warm food, good conversation, and space to bring new friends.

Living in or around Nijmegen? We also organize and recommend gatherings there when possible.
