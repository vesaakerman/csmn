## CSMN - Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is CSMN?

CSMN stands for Chinese Student Ministry Netherlands. We are a Christian organisation for Chinese students and are officially linked to the International Christian Fellowship (ICF).

### What do we do?

We create a safe and welcoming space where students can explore faith, ask meaningful questions, build community, and grow together. This includes regular meetings, discussions, worship, social events, and occasional trips or retreats.

### What do we not do?

We do not force anyone to follow Jesus. We respect everyone's journey and beliefs.

### Why specifically Chinese students?

Our team has a special bond with China. Most of us have lived there and feel a deep connection to the culture and people. We want Chinese students to feel seen, understood, and at home in a country that can feel very different from their own.

### I am not a Christian. Can I join?

Yes. We welcome students from all backgrounds and worldviews, whether you are curious, skeptical, or simply want to explore faith and ask big questions.

### What is the cost for membership?

There is no cost to join. Our events and meetings are free unless otherwise specified for special events or trips.

### What can I expect?

We aim for a friendly and respectful environment. We do not drink alcohol or smoke during our gatherings or events.

### What activities do you do besides regular meetings?

In addition to weekly gatherings, we organise hikes, nature trips, movie nights, social dinners, game nights, weekend retreats, and camps.

### How does a typical evening look?

A usual evening includes welcome and informal hangout, singing or worship with optional participation, a short talk or discussion, group conversation or reflection, and food or snacks with social time.

### Are you only for Protestants?

While we are a Protestant Christian organisation, we warmly welcome students from all denominations and beliefs. Everyone is invited to join and be part of the community.
