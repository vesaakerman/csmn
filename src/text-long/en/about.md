## CSMN: frequently asked questions

**Who is CSMN?**  
CSMN stands for Chinese Student Ministry Netherlands. We are a Christian organization for Chinese students and are linked to the International Christian Fellowship.

**What do we do?**  
We create a safe and welcoming space where students can explore faith, ask meaningful questions, build community, and grow together. This includes regular meetings, discussions, worship, social events, and occasional trips.

**What do we not do?**  
We do not force anyone to follow Jesus. We respect everyone’s journey and beliefs.

**Why specifically Chinese students?**  
Our team has a special bond with China. Most of us have lived there and feel a deep connection to the culture and people. We want Chinese students to feel seen, understood, and at home in a country that can feel very different.

**I am not a Christian. Can I join?**  
Yes. We welcome students from all backgrounds and worldviews, whether you are curious, skeptical, or simply looking for community.
