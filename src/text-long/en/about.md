## Welcome to Chinese Student Ministries Netherlands (CSMN)

CSMN is a community for Chinese university students in the Netherlands, with a special focus on those living and studying in Wageningen. We provide a welcoming and open space where students can connect, build friendships, and explore meaningful questions about life, culture, and faith.

## What We Offer

In addition to our weekly fellowship gatherings, where we share a Chinese meal and engage in discussions guided by the Bible, we also organize a wide range of events throughout the year. These events are designed to be both thoughtful and enjoyable, because life is serious, but also fun!

Examples of past and upcoming activities include:

- BBQs & Hot Pot evenings
- Cross-cultural exchange nights
- "Life Explored" sessions
- Leadership development workshop
- Hikes in and around Wageningen
- Chestnut picking in autumn
- City trips to explore the Netherlands

And many more! Whether you are looking for a place to grow personally, spiritually, or socially, you are warmly invited to join us. [Find out more about our events here!](/en/events)
